# ⭐ That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime  
*A fantasy isekai adventure with diplomacy, evolution, and monsters.*

## 📘 Overview
**That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime** (*Tensei Shitara Slime Datta Ken*) is a popular Japanese light novel-turned-anime that tells the story of a man reborn as a slime in a magical world.  

> *"Even a slime can become the strongest."*

- **Original Author:** Fuse  
- **Illustrator:** Mitz Vah  
- **First Episode Aired:** October 2, 2018  
- **Genre:** Isekai, Fantasy, Action  

---

## 🔥 Plot Summary  
Satoru Mikami, a 37-year-old salaryman in Tokyo, is fatally stabbed and reincarnated as a slime in a fantasy world.

He gains extraordinary powers like:  
- `Predator` – to absorb and mimic skills  
- `Great Sage` – an AI-like voice that gives tactical advice  
- `Magic Sense` – lets him see everything in a 360° range  

He evolves into **Rimuru Tempest**, forms alliances, and creates the *Jura Tempest Federation*—a peaceful monster nation.
